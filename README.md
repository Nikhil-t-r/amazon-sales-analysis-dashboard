This dashboard provides a detailed analysis of Amazon sales performance using interactive visualizations and KPI cards. It focuses on key business metrics such as Total Sales, Total Profit, Total Quantity Sold, and Customer Ratings, helping users understand overall business performance and customer satisfaction.

The dashboard enables users to explore sales trends, profitability, product demand, and customer feedback through data-driven insights, making it easier to identify patterns and support business decisions.

Key Metrics Covered:

Total Sales
Total Profit
Total Quantity Sold
Customer Rating

Tools Used:

Power BI
Microsoft Excel

Objective:
To analyze Amazon sales data and present meaningful insights through an interactive dashboard. This project demonstrates data cleaning, data visualization, and business intelligence skills using Power BI to support data-driven decision-making.
